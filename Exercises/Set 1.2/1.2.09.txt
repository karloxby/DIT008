		b. Is 1 ⊆ {1}?
		c. Is {2} ∈ {1, 2}?
		d. Is {3} ∈ {1, {2}, {3}}?
		e. Is 1 ∈ {1}?
		f. Is {2} ⊆ {1, {2}, {3}}?
		g. Is {1} ⊆ {1, 2}?
		h. Is 1 ∈ {{1}, 2}?
		i. Is {1} ⊆ {1, {2}}?
		j. Is {1} ⊆ {1}?


		a. Is 3 ∈ {1, 2, 3}?

Solution:	Yes, 3 is an element of the set.


		b. Is 1 ⊆ {1}?

Solution:	No, 1 is not a subset of {1}.


		c. Is {2} ∈ {1, 2}?

Solution:	No, {2} is not an element of the set.


		d. Is {3} ∈ {1, {2}, {3}}?
		
Solution:	Yes, {3} is an element of the set.


		e. Is 1 ∈ {1}?

Solution:	Yes, 1 is an element of the set.


		f. Is {2} ⊆ {1, {2}, {3}}?

Solution:	No, {2} is not a subset of the set.


		g. Is {1} ⊆ {1, 2}?

Solution:	Yes, {1} is a subset of the set.


		h. Is 1 ∈ {{1}, 2}?

Solution:	No, 1 is not an element of the set.


		i. Is {1} ⊆ {1, {2}}?

Solution:	Yes, {1} is a subset of the set.


		j. Is {1} ⊆ {1}?
		
Solution:	Yes, {1} is a subset of the set.
