1.2.15.		Let S = {0, 1}. List all the strings of length 4 over
		S that contains three or more 0's.

		
		Suppose S = {0, 1}

		We must list all strings of length 4 over S that
		contains three or more 0's.

		Let elements in S = 2
		Let length of strings = 4

		2⁴ = 16

		There are 16 possible combinations of strings
		of length 4 over S.

		0000, 0001, 0011, 0111 
		0101, 0100, 0010, 1001
		1010, 1011, 1101, 0110
		1111, 1110, 1100, 1000

		Since S = {0, 1}, then a string over S is called a
		bit string.

Solution:	0000, 0001, 0100, 0010, 1000
