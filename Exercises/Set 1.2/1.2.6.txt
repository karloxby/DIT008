1.2.6		For each integer n, let Tₙ = {n, n²}. How many
		elements are in each of T₂, T₋₃, T₁ and T₀? Justify
		your answers.


		T₋₃ = {(-3), (-3)²} = {-3, 9}

Solution:	T₋₃ contains two elements: -3 and 9.


		T₀ = {0, 0²} = {0, 0}
		
Solution:	T₀ contains one element: 0.


		T₁ = {1, 1²}

Solution:	T₁ contains one element: 1.


		T₂ = {2, 2²}

Solution:	T₂ contains two elements: 2 and 4.
