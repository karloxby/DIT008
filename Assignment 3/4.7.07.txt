Carefully formulate the negations of each of the statements
in 5-7. Then prove each statement by contradiction.


4.7.4.		There is no least positive rational number.


		Proof:

			The given statement claims that
			there is no least positive rational
			number.

			This statement is difficult to prove,
			since we have nothing to work with.

			But we can negate the statement, and then
			prove by contradiction.

			Suppose not. Suppose there is a least
			positive rational number.

			Let r, R ∈ ℚ⁺, where r is the least
			positive rational number.

			We must show that there is a positive
			rational number R, such that 0 < R < r.
			
			Let r = k / i, for some k, i ∈ ℤ, i ≠ 0.
			Let R = r / 2

			Therefore, 0 < R < r, and this is what
			was to be shown by contradiction.

								□

Solution:	There is no least positive rational number.
