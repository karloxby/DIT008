4.7.4.		Use proof by contradiction to show that for every
		integer m, 7m + 4 is not divisible by 7.

		
		Given statement is that ∀m ∈ ℤ, 7∤(7m + 4)

		Proof:

			Suppose not. Suppose m is some integer.

			We must show that 7m + 4 is divisible by 7.

				∃m ∈ ℤ st 7 ∣ 7m + 4

			Definition of divisibility:
			
				d ∣ n ↔ ∃k ∈ ℤ such that n = dk

			Let m ∈ ℤ

			d = 7
			n = 7m + 4

			7 | 7m + 4 ↔ 7m + 4 = 7k, for some k ∈ ℤ

				7m = 7k - 4
				4 = 7k - 7m = 7(k - m)

				4 / 7 = k - m

			Since k - m ∈ ℤ, but 4 / 7 ∉ ℤ, the statement
			is true by proof by contradiction, which is
			what was to be shown.

								□


Solution:	Therefore, 7m + 4 is not divisible by 7.
