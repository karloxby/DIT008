4.4.28.		For all integers a, b and c, if a|bc then a|b or a|c.

		Suppose a, b and c ∈ Z.

		We must show that if a is divisible by bc, then
		a is divisible by b or a is divisible by c.

		Argument form:
		∀a, b, c ∈ Z, a|bc → a|b ∨ a|c

		"For all integers a, b and c, if a divides bc,
		then a divides b or a divides c."

		Negation:
		∃a, b, c ∈ Z st a|bc ∧ ~(a|b) ∧ ~(a|c)

		"There are integers a, b and c, such that
		a divides bc, a does not divide b, and a
		does not divide c."

		Definition of divisibility:

		"n is divisible by d ↔ n = dk, for some integer
		k, and d ≠ 0."
		
		Let a = 6
		Let b = 3
		Let c = 2
		
		bc = 6

			a|bc = bc / a = 6 / 6 = 1 ∈ Z

		Therefore, a|bc.

			a|b = b / a = 3 / 6 = 1 / 2 ∉ Z
			a|c = 2 / 6 = 1 / 3 ∉ Z

		Therefore, a∤b, and a∤c.


Solution:	Since a|bc, but a∤b and a∤c, the satement is false
		by counterexample.
