4.4.39.		Suppose that in standard factored form
		a = p₁ᵉ¹p₂ᵉ²...pₖᵉᵏ, where k is a positive integer: p₁,
		p₂, ..., pₖ are prime numbers; and e₁, e₂, ..., eₖ 
		are positive integers.

		a. What is the standard factored form for a³?

		b. Find the least positive integer k such that
		2⁴ * 3⁵ * 7 * 11² * k is a perfect cube (that is, it
		equals an integer to the third power). Write the
		resulting product as a perfect cube.


		a.	What is the standard factored form for a³?
		
			a = p₁ᵉ¹p₂ᵉ²...pₖᵉᵏ
			a³ = (p₁ᵉ¹p₂ᵉ²...pₖᵉᵏ)³ = p₁³ᵉ¹p₂³ᵉ²...pₖ³ᵉᵏ

Solution:	a³ = p₁³ᵉ¹p₂³ᵉ²...pₖ³ᵉᵏ

		
		b.	Find the least positive integer k such that
			2⁴ * 3⁵ * 7 * 11² * k is a perfect cube (that is, it
			equals an integer to the third power). Write the
			resulting product as a perfect cube.
			
			a³ = 2⁴ * 3⁵ * 7 * 11² * k

			2⁴ * 2² = 2⁶
			3⁵ * 3¹ = 3⁶
			7¹ * 7² = 7³
			11² * 11¹ = 11³

			k = 2² * 3¹ * 7² * 11¹

			a³ = 2⁴ * 3⁵ * 7 * 11² * (2² * 3¹ * 7² * 11¹)
			a³ = 2⁶ * 3⁶ * 7³ * 11³

			a³ = (2² * 3² * 7 * 11)³

Solution:	k = 6468
