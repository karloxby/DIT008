7.2.34.		Prove that for all positive real numbers a, x, and y
		with a ≠ 1,

			logₐ (xy) = logₐ x + logₐ y

		(Note: Substituted subscript b for a)


		Proof:

		Suppose a, x, and y are positive real numbers, with
		a ≠ 1.

		We must show that logₐ (xy) = logₐ x + logₐ y.

		Let a, x, y ∈ ℝ⁺, a ≠ 1

		Definition of Logarithm and Logarithmic functions:

			logₙ r = s ↔ nˢ = r

		Let z, u, v ∈ ℝ | u = logₐ x, v = logₐ y, and 
		logₐ (xy) = z, then:

			logₐ (xy) = z ↔ aᶻ = xy
			
			logₐ x = u ↔ aᵘ = x
			logₐ y = v ↔ aᵛ = y

		Hence, aᶻ = xy, aᵘ = x and aᵛ = y. Then:

			xy = aᵘaᵛ = aᵘ⁺ᵛ
			xy = aᵘ⁺ᵛ

			xy = aᶻ
			aᶻ = aᵘ⁺ᵛ

		Therefore, z = u + v, and since z = logₐ (xy),
		u = logₐ x, and v = logₐ y, then logₐ (xy) = 
		logₐ x + logₐ y, which what was to be shown.

								□

Solution:	for all positive real numbers a, x, and y with a ≠ 1,
		logₐ (xy) = logₐ x + logₐ y is true.
