7.1.24.		If a and y are positive real numbers such that
		logₐ y = 2, what is logₐ² (y)? Explain.

		(Note, could not use subscript b, so used a)


		Definition Logarithms and Logarithmic Functions:

			logₐ x = y ↔ aʸ = x

		Let a, y ∈ ℝ⁺

			logₐ y = 2 ↔ a² = y
			
				a² = y

			logₐ² (y) = x ↔ (a²)ˣ = y
			logₐ² (a²) = x ↔ (a²)ˣ = a²

				(a²)ˣ = a²ˣ
				a²ˣ = a²

				x = 1

		Hence, logₐ² y = 1

Solution:	logₐ² y = 1
