7.1.2.		Let X = {1, 3, 5} and Y = {a, b, c, d}. Define
		g : X → Y by the following arrow diagram.

		X	g	Y

		1	→	b
		3	→	b
		5	→	b


		Suppose X is the domain and Y is the co-domain for
		function g.

		We must define g : X → Y

		g satisfies two properties:

			(1)	Every element in X is related
				to some element in Y.

			(2)	No element in X is related to
				more than one element in Y.
		
		X is the domain and Y is the co-domain.

			- domain of g = {1, 3, 5}
			- co-domain of g = {a, b, c, d}

		g(1) = b, g(3) = b, and g(5) = b

			Range of g = image of X under g = {y ∈ Y | g(x) = y for some x ∈ X}

		Hence, range of g = {b}

Solution:	g(1) = b, g(3) = b, g(5) = b
		Domain of g = {1, 3, 5}
		Co-domain of g = {a, b, c, d}
		Range of g = {b}
