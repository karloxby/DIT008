40.	Being divisible by 8 is a sufficient condition
	for being divisible by 4.

	Let's look at the definition of a sufficient condition
	according to Epp:

	"For all x, r(x) is a SUFFICIENT CONDITION for s(x)"
	means "For all x, if r(x) then s(x)"

	Now, let's look at what the initial statement again:

	"Being divisible by 8 is a sufficient condition
	for being divisible by 4."
	
	Let n be an integer
	Then for all integers n, if 8 | n, then 4 | n.	

	(A) n in Z, if 8 | n -> 4 | n.

	And in English:

	"For all integers n, if n is divisible by 8, then
	n is also divisible by 4."

	Since the assignment was to rewrite the statement, not
	prove or disprove it, we can therefore conclude that
	we are now done.


Solution:	(A) n in Z, if 8 | n -> 4 | n
