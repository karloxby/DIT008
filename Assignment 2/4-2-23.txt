23.	The product of any even integer and any integer is
	even.


	Proof:

		Suppose that we have any even integer, and any
		integer, and suppose both numbers are particular,
		but arbitrarily chosen.

		We must prove that the product is even.

		The definition of an even number according to
		Epp:

			"n is even <=> n = 2k for some integer k"

		The definition of an odd number according to
		Epp:

			"n is odd <=> n = 2k + 1 for some integer k"

		Let's look at the given statement:

		"The product of any even integer and any integer
		is even."

		Let a, b, x, y in Z

		a = 2x
		b = y

		ab = 2xy

		Since 2xy fulfills the definition of an even
		number, and xy is an integer, the statement is
		true.

		Therefore, the product of any even integer
		and any integer is even.
							(Proof)


Solution:	The statement is true.
