10.	Assume that m and n are both integers and that
	n ≠ 0. Explain why (5m - 12n) / (4n) must be a
	rational number.

	
	Suppose m and n are both integers and that
	n ≠ 0.

	We must explain why (5m - 12n) / (4n) must be
	a rational number.

	Let's look at the definition of a rational number
	according to Epp:

		"r is rational <=> (E) a, b in Z, such that
		r = a / b and b ≠ 0"
	
	Since (5m - 12n) is an integer, and 4n is an
	integer, where n ≠ 0, (5m - 12n) / (4n) is
	a quotient of the integers (5m - 12n) and 4n,
	and hence is rational, because it fulfills the
	definition of a rational number.

	Therefore, (5m - 12n) / (4n) is rational.


Solution:	Yes, (5m - 12n) / (4n) is rational.
