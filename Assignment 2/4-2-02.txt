2.	For every integer m, if m is even then 3m + 5 is
	odd.

	Proof:

		Suppose m is a particular but arbitrarily chosen
		integer, and suppose m is even.

		We have to prove that 3m + 5 is odd.

		Let's look at the definitions of an even, and
		respectively an odd number according to Epp:

		n is even <=> n = 2k for some integer k
		n is odd <=> n = 2k + 1 for some integer k
		
			Let k in Z.

			m = 2k

			3m + 5 = 3(2k) + 5 = 6k + 5

			6k + 5 can be written as:

				2(3k + 2) + 1

		2(3k + 2) + 1 fulfills the definition of an
		odd number, since 3k + 2 is also an integer.

		Therefore, 3m + 5 is odd.

							(Proof)


Solution:	(A)m in Z, if m is even, then 3m + 5 is odd.
