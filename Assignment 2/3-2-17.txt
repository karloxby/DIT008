In 16-23, write a negation for each statement.


17.	(A) integer d, if b / d is an integer, then d = 3.

	
	Let d in Z, and
	let P(d) be 6 / d is an integer, and
	let Q(d) be d = 3

	(A) d in Z, P(d) -> Q(d)

	Then we negate it:

	~((A)d in Z,) is equivalent to:
		(E) d in Z s.t.
	
	~(P(d) -> Q(d)) is equivalent to:
		P(d) ^ ~Q(d)

	And if we put it together:

	(E) d in Z s.t. P(d) ^ ~Q(d)

	In English:

	"There is at least one integer d, such that
	P(d) and ~Q(d)."

	Let's pick a particular integer d:

		Let d = 1
		6 / 1 = 6 in Z
		d ≠ 3

	Therefore, the statement is true.


Solution:	(E) d in Z s.t. 6 / d is an integer, and d ≠ 3.
