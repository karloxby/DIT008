Proof by contradiction:

ARGUMENT BY CONTRAPOSITION

Proof by Contraposition:

	(1)	Express the statement to be proven
		in the form ∀x ∈ D, P(x) → Q(x)

	(2)	Rewrite the statement in its
		contrapositive

		∀x ∈ D, ~Q(x) → ~P(x)
	
	(3)	Prove the contrapositive by direct
		proof.

		(a)	Let x ∈ D st Q(x) is false
		(b)	Show that P(x) is false


PROPOSITION (4.7.4)

For every integer n, if n² is even, then n is even.

∀n ∈ ℤ, n² is even → n is even

Proof:

	Prove the contrapositive:

	∀n ∈ ℤ odd, n² is odd

	Let n ∈ Z be odd. Then ∃k ∈ Z st n = 2k + 1

	Hence, n² = (2k + 1)² = 4k² + 4k + 1 =
	= 2(2k² + 2k) + 1

	Since 2k² + 2k ∈ Z, n² is odd
			
					□
